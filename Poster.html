⚙️ Research Methodology

Research Context: This study applies computational methods to analyze the semiotic elements present in Assamese film posters, contributing to the understanding of regional cinema's visual language and cultural representation.

Three-Stage Analysis Pipeline:

  1. Data Curation: Collection and annotation of Assamese film posters with structured semiotic metadata (Sign, Representation, Meaning).
  2. Model Training: Comparative evaluation of CNN architectures (ResNet, VGG, EfficientNet) for multi-label semiotic element classification.
  3. Cultural Analysis: Extraction of visual patterns, color symbolism, and compositional elements specific to Assamese cinematic tradition.

📚 Theoretical Framework

This analysis draws from Barthesian semiotics and Peircean sign theory, applying computational vision to decode cultural narratives embedded in Assamese cinema's visual artifacts. The methodology bridges traditional film studies with contemporary machine learning techniques.

💡 Cultural & Semiotic Insights

🎨 Visual Language of Assamese Cinema

The computational analysis reveals distinct visual patterns in Assamese film posters:

  • Color Symbolism: Predominance of earth tones and vibrant primary colors reflecting cultural aesthetics
  • Compositional Patterns: Strategic use of focal points to emphasize narrative elements
  • Cultural Motifs: Integration of traditional Assamese symbols and contemporary design elements
  • Textual Integration: Bilingual typography reflecting linguistic identity

🔬 Methodological Contributions

This study demonstrates the viability of deep learning for cultural film studies, offering:

  • Scalable framework for analyzing large poster archives
  • Quantitative metrics for visual cultural studies
  • Cross-architecture comparison for domain-specific tasks
  • Interpretable AI for humanities research
